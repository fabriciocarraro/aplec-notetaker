Aplec

The self-hosted AI notetaker for your meetings — private by architecture.

Aplec joins your Google Meet meetings automatically, records them, and turns each one into structured notes: a summary, key points, decisions, and action items with owners — plus a full speaker-labelled, multilingual transcript you can search and ask questions about. All processing (speech recognition, speaker identification, summarization) runs on your own server. No meeting audio or content is ever sent to a third-party AI service.

How it works

1 · Connect your Google Calendar

Sign in with Google and grant calendar access. Aplec watches your upcoming events for Google Meet links.

2 · Aplec joins by itself

Moments before each meeting starts, Aplec adds its notetaker account as a guest and joins the call as a visible participant.

3 · Notes appear minutes after the call

Summary, action items, decisions, and a speaker-labelled transcript — in the languages actually spoken, side by side.

Why Aplec needs calendar access

Aplec requests the calendar.events permission for exactly two things: reading your upcoming events to find Google Meet meetings to join, and adding its own notetaker account as a guest on those events so it can enter the meeting as an invited participant. Aplec never creates, deletes, or otherwise modifies your events.

Private by architecture

  • Speech-to-text, speaker ID, and summaries are computed with open-source models on self-hosted hardware.
  • Meeting audio is deleted automatically after 15 days; notes stay until you delete them.
  • Access is restricted to an administrator-managed allowlist; each user sees only their own meetings.
